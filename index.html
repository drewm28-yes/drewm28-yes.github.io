Historical Thinking Skills

The Four Core Skills

C
Contextualization
Situate events within the broader regional, national, or global context of their specific era — 3-4 sentences, before your thesis.
C
Causation
Identify and analyze both short-term and long-term causes and effects. Always ask: what caused this, and what did it cause?
C
Comparison
Describe, compare, and evaluate multiple historical developments — within one society or between different societies and time periods.
C
CCOT
Recognize, analyze, and evaluate historical continuity and change over specified time periods — what persisted, what transformed, and when?

Essay Types

DBQ
Document-Based Question
7 documents · 7 points possible · 60 min
Analyze 7 primary/secondary source documents to write an argument. Must include: thesis, contextualization, evidence from docs (3+) AND outside knowledge, and at least one HAPP (Historical Argument and Purpose, Perspective) analysis.
LEQ
Long Essay Question
Choose 1 of 3 · 6 points · 40 min
Write a well-organized argument using specific historical evidence. Must demonstrate: thesis with line of reasoning, contextualization, evidence beyond description, and a complex understanding of the historical development.
SAQ
Short Answer Question
3 questions · 9 points total · 40 min
Three separate questions each with parts A, B, and C. No thesis required — but each answer must be supported by specific historical evidence. Tests all periods with stimulus-based and non-stimulus prompts.
